01 / Introduction

About me

A statistics foundation, a growing focus on artificial intelligence, and a commitment to rigorous analysis.

01

I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf, Germany. I previously completed a B.Sc. in Statistics at Begum Rokeya University, Rangpur, Bangladesh.

My interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, statistical inference, forecasting and applied research. I am particularly interested in using reproducible analytical methods to study complex real-world data, including public health data.

My experience includes research support, data analysis and peer-review activities. I also enjoy developing practical software projects alongside academic work.
