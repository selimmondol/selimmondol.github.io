01 / Academic profile

Academic Profile

Bridging statistical foundations with artificial intelligence and interdisciplinary research.

01

Md Selim Mondol is currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf, Germany. He holds a B.Sc. in Statistics from Begum Rokeya University, Rangpur, Bangladesh.

His academic interests focus on machine learning, statistical modelling, and computational methods for addressing complex real-world challenges. His research and academic projects span public health analytics, infectious disease forecasting, network science, and conversational artificial intelligence, with an emphasis on methodological rigour, reproducibility, and reliable model evaluation.
